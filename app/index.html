Milestones & reviewers

One reviewer generates a different secret for each stage.

Epochs are chain numbers, not calendar dates. Confirm the current epoch and leave enough time for review and claims. Targets, milestone order, and reviewer independence are not enforced by the native escrow.

Agree before
anyone pledges.

  1. Each independent reviewer creates a secret on their own device.
  2. They share its hashlock only for the agreement.
  3. Everyone checks the recipient, work, and refund epoch.
  4. The creator distributes the same public terms.
Keep secrets out of public terms.

Never paste a reviewer preimage, wallet seed, refund mask, or recovery file into campaign descriptions.

INDEPENDENT REVIEW, EXPLICIT APPROVAL

Keep the agreement.

A reviewer holds the release secret. The named creator uses that secret to claim a pledge before its refund epoch.

FOR REVIEWERS

A secret for one milestone.

Generate this on the reviewer’s own device. Share only the hashlock while planning. Revealing the secret enables the creator to claim every pledge with that hashlock.

Keep the secret file offline.

Save it before closing this page. Generate a new secret for each milestone. Losing it prevents reviewer-authorized claims.

FOR PROJECT CREATORS

Claim an approved pledge.

Import the donor’s claim handoff privately. This contains the exact conditions your wallet needs. The refund mask stays with the donor.

Claims disclose the preimage to Sapient’s indexer during preflight and then on-chain. The claimant signer is also visible. Check the amount and conditions in Sapient.

Recovery has
requirements.

An eligible unpaid pledge can be refunded at or after its chain refund epoch. Already claimed funds cannot be reclaimed.

A wallet seed alone is not enough.

Keep the encrypted recovery file, its password, the original wallet, and fee funds. The conditions and refund mask cannot be reconstructed from the chain alone.

New pledges are disabled in this prototype. This tool supports existing compatible recovery files and does not demonstrate a completed live refund.
